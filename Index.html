FAQ

Questions before the diagnostic call.

Which jurisdictions do you work in?+

We serve clients incorporated in the US, and companies with a US entity paired with an Indian, UK, or Singapore subsidiary. Our technical accounting covers US GAAP, IFRS, and Ind AS. If your structure sits outside those jurisdictions, tell us on the diagnostic call and we'll say plainly whether we're the right fit.

Can you handle both our audit and our monthly accounting?+

Not at the same time, and not for the same fiscal year. Independence standards require that we not audit books we also maintain. Most clients pick one or the other with us; some rotate audit providers so we can run their advisory work continuously.

How is pricing structured?+

A fixed monthly retainer, scoped to your entity count, transaction volume, and reporting cadence in the engagement letter. Audit and one-off projects — like a PPA or an R&D credit study — are quoted separately and fixed before work starts.

What accounting systems do you work inside?+

QuickBooks Online, NetSuite, and Xero most often, along with Bill.com, Ramp, Ramp Payroll, Gusto, and Ind AS-compliant systems like Tally and Zoho Books for Indian subsidiaries. We work inside your existing stack rather than migrating you to ours.

How quickly can an engagement start?+

Most clients move from diagnostic call to signed scope within two weeks, and see their first monthly close delivered on our calendar within 30–45 days — sooner if books are already current.

Do you work with companies that don't have a controller yet?+

That's most of our client base. We typically step in as the first dedicated finance function a company has had, and either hand off to an internal hire later or stay embedded indefinitely — whichever the business needs.
